How it’s counted

Solio Analytics (@robtfpl) publishes each team’s projected goals from sharp betting markets before every gameweek; these are the final updates. To judge them like a scoreline, each team’s projected goals is rounded down to a whole number, so 1.84 v 0.95 becomes 1–0 and 2.67 v 0.53 becomes 2–0. Results, exact scores, backed the wrong team, clean sheets, teams scoring 2+ and the league table all use that rounded-down scoreline: a clean sheet is called when the opponent is projected under 1 goal, and 2+ is called when a team is projected 2 goals or more. Solio’s clean sheet percentages aren’t used.

Projections were copied from Solio’s weekly graphics. Results are full-time scores. Match xG comes from xG Stat; gameweek 2 and Newcastle v Everton aren’t included yet, so they show a dash and are left out of the xG comparison.
